Scope & Priorities

What's included in the initial campaign push vs. future enhancements

🎯 Launch Principles

  • Ship to learn: Launch with core value, iterate based on real user feedback
  • Speed over perfection: Better to launch in March with 80% than June with 100%
  • Data-driven enhancement: Let performance guide what to build next, not assumptions
  • Reusable assets: Everything we create should be repurposable across channels
  • Partner-ready: Prioritize assets that enable partner amplification from day one
Core Mantra:

Launch lean. Measure everything. Double down on what works. This campaign is Phase 1 of a multi-phase content hub strategy—not a one-and-done project.

✅ In Scope: Initial Campaign Push

These deliverables are part of the March campaign launch and required for success.

📄 Hub Project

  • Comprehensive content expansion
  • New hub layout/structure
  • UX and design refresh
  • SEO optimization
  • Internal linking structure
  • Clear CTAs to course/checklist

🎓 Course/Checklist

  • 5-7 module course OR interactive checklist
  • Text-based content with actionable steps
  • Downloadable templates/resources
  • Progress tracking functionality
  • Expert insights from Jonathan
  • Email sequences for engagement

📝 Blog Content (Seeding)

  • 4-6 SEO-optimized articles
  • Published pre-launch for topic seeding
  • All linking back to hub
  • Targeting key pain points

📱 Organic Social

  • 15-20 pre-written social posts
  • Graphics and quote cards
  • LinkedIn carousels (3-5)
  • Post schedule/calendar

🎥 Video Content (Initial)

  • 1-2 hero videos for hub/course intro
  • 3-5 short clips (30-60s) for social/ads
  • Featuring Jonathan or expert
  • Multiple format exports (square, vertical, horizontal)

💰 Paid Ads

  • 3-5 video ad variations
  • Performance Max campaign setup
  • Multiple ad copy variations
  • Landing page optimization

🤝 Partner Assets

  • Partner toolkit (social posts, graphics, email templates)
  • Co-branded graphics
  • UTM tracking links
  • Partner commitments secured

📧 Email Sequences

  • Welcome series (3-5 emails)
  • Progress nudges
  • Re-engagement campaign
  • Webinar follow-up (if webinar runs)

💻 Webinar (Optional)

  • 30-45 min live or pre-recorded session
  • With Jonathan or expert
  • Timed with campaign launch
  • Slide deck and recording

🔮 Out of Scope: Future Enhancements

These are valuable additions but NOT required for initial launch. They will be prioritized based on campaign performance and learnings.

📊 Case Studies

  • Detailed customer success stories
  • Video testimonials
  • Problem-solution-result narratives
  • Why later: Can enhance hub credibility, but not critical for initial conversion

📘 Buyers Guide

  • Comprehensive "how to choose" resource
  • Comparison frameworks
  • Decision-making tools
  • Why later: Valuable for SEO but requires significant research/writing

🎥 Extensive Video Library

  • Video for every course module
  • Multiple expert interviews
  • Customer video testimonials
  • Why later: Nice-to-have but doubles production time and cost

📰 PR & Media Outreach

  • Press releases
  • Industry publication pitches
  • Deep research reports (e.g., "State of Private Practice 2025")
  • Why later: Better to do once we have performance data and strong customer stories

🌍 Additional Expert Contributions

  • Guest articles from multiple experts
  • Expert roundtable discussions
  • Industry leader interviews
  • Why later: Jonathan provides initial credibility; expand expert network based on engagement

📚 Supporting Resources Library

  • Template library (documents, policies, forms)
  • Toolkit downloads
  • Regulatory checklists beyond CQC
  • Why later: Valuable additions but can be added incrementally post-launch
Decision Framework:

Post-launch, we'll prioritize enhancements based on: 1) What users request most, 2) What drives highest conversion, 3) What partners say they need, 4) What data shows is missing. We're not saying "no" to these—we're saying "not yet."

📊 Success Criteria for Initial Launch

These metrics will determine whether we've successfully launched and what to enhance next.

  • Hub traffic: 1,000+ unique visitors in first month post-launch
  • Course enrollments: 200+ sign-ups in first month
  • Course completion: 30%+ completion rate for enrolled users
  • SEO performance: Hub ranking on page 1 for 3+ target keywords within 3 months
  • Partner engagement: 5+ partners actively sharing campaign assets
  • Email engagement: 30%+ open rate, 5%+ click rate on email sequences
  • Ad performance: Sub-£20 cost per course enrollment on paid ads
:::
