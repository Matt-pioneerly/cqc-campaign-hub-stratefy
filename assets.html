Campaign Assets Guide

How to create high-value assets for maximum impact

🏠

Content Hub

Evergreen Resource Center

What It Is

A comprehensive, SEO-optimized landing page that serves as the central resource for a specific topic (e.g., "How to Launch a Private Practice"). It aggregates blog posts, guides, tools, and other resources into one navigable destination.

How to Make It Valuable

  • Comprehensive structure: Cover all major subtopics with clear navigation
  • Internal linking: Connect to relevant blog posts, guides, and campaign assets
  • Progressive depth: Start with overview content, link to deeper dives
  • Regular updates: Keep content current with latest regulations, trends, data
  • Clear CTAs: Guide visitors to campaign assets (course, checklist) at natural points
  • Visual hierarchy: Use headers, cards, and sections for easy scanning
Best Practice: Design the hub to answer common questions at different stages of the journey. Each section should link to either a blog post (for awareness) or a campaign asset (for conversion).
🎓

Course / Interactive Checklist

Lead Magnet & Conversion Asset

What It Is

A structured, actionable resource that guides users through a specific process (e.g., CQC registration). Can be a multi-module course, an interactive checklist with guidance, or a combination of both.

How to Make It Valuable

  • Modular structure: Break into digestible steps (5-7 modules ideal)
  • Actionable content: Every section should have clear next steps
  • Templates included: Provide downloadable documents, checklists, worksheets
  • Progress tracking: Show users how far they've come and what's left
  • Expert credibility: Feature insights from Jonathan or other experts
  • Mixed media: Combine text, video, downloadables for different learning styles
  • Community element: If using Circle.so, enable peer discussion
Best Practice: Start with text-based modules for speed, then add video and interactivity over time. Each module should end with a clear "what to do next" action.
🎥

Video Content

Multi-Purpose Promotional Asset

What It Is

Short-form (2-5 min) or long-form (10-15 min) video content featuring Jonathan or experts explaining key concepts, common pitfalls, or success stories related to private practice launch.

How to Make It Valuable

  • Modular shooting: Film multiple topics in one session for efficiency
  • Repurpose-first mindset: Structure for easy cutting into shorter clips
  • Strong hooks: First 3-5 seconds must grab attention for social
  • Subtitles always: Essential for social media consumption
  • Multiple formats: Horizontal (YouTube), square (LinkedIn/FB), vertical (Stories/Shorts)
  • CTA at end: Direct viewers to hub or course enrollment
  • B-roll and graphics: Keep visually engaging, avoid talking heads only
Best Practice: Create video with three uses in mind: 1) embedded in course/hub, 2) ads on social, 3) organic social posts. Script and shoot accordingly.
💰

Paid Ads (Video)

Performance Max & Social Campaign Assets

What It Is

Video-first ads designed for Google Performance Max campaigns, YouTube, LinkedIn, and Facebook. Typically 15-60 seconds, optimized to drive course enrollments or hub visits. Can also include display ads with static images or carousels.

How to Make It Valuable

  • Hook in 3 seconds: Problem statement or bold claim to stop scroll
  • Multiple lengths: Cut each video into 15s, 30s, and 60s versions
  • Problem-Solution-CTA: Clear narrative arc for conversion-focused ads
  • Subtitles mandatory: 85% of social video watched without sound
  • Test variations: Different hooks, CTAs, and messaging angles
  • Platform-specific specs: Square for Feed, vertical for Stories, horizontal for YouTube
  • Thumbnail optimization: Design for small screens and mobile viewing
  • Performance Max assets: Provide multiple headlines, descriptions, images, and videos for Google's algorithm
Best Practice: For Performance Max, provide at least 5 different video variations (different hooks, angles) so Google's algorithm can test and optimize. Always include a clear CTA directing to course landing page.
🔗

Shareable Assets

Partner & Internal Distribution Materials

What It Is

Ready-to-share content packages designed for partners, contributors, and internal teams to amplify the campaign. Includes pre-written social posts, email templates, graphics, video clips, and landing page links.

How to Make It Valuable

  • Partner kits: Provide complete package with social posts, email copy, graphics, UTM links
  • Multiple formats: LinkedIn posts, Twitter threads, email templates, Instagram stories
  • Personalization fields: Make it easy to add partner's name/brand without rewriting
  • Value props per audience: Tailor messaging for different partner audiences
  • UTM tracking: Unique links per partner to measure their contribution
  • Co-branding templates: Easy to add partner logos alongside Semble
  • Video snippets: 15-30s clips partners can share directly or re-upload
  • Internal sharing kit: Pre-approved messaging for team members to share on personal profiles
  • Schedule suggestions: Recommend optimal posting times and cadence
Best Practice: Create a simple Notion page or Google Drive folder with all assets organized by type. Include copy-paste social posts with [PARTNER NAME] placeholders. Make it ridiculously easy to share in under 2 minutes.
📝

Blog Content

SEO & Awareness Driver

What It Is

SEO-optimized articles (800-1500 words) addressing specific questions or pain points related to launching a private practice. Serves as top-of-funnel content to drive organic traffic.

How to Make It Valuable

  • Keyword research: Target long-tail queries with clear search intent
  • Answer-first format: Get to the point quickly, expand with detail
  • Internal linking: Link to hub and related blog posts (3-5 internal links minimum)
  • Clear CTA: At end, direct to course/checklist or hub for "next steps"
  • Expert quotes: Include Jonathan's insights for credibility
  • Scannable format: Use headers, bullets, short paragraphs
  • Regular publishing: Consistency matters for SEO (2-4 posts/month)
Best Practice: Write blogs with hub structure in mind. Each blog should naturally fit into a hub category and link back to it. Think of blogs as "spokes" feeding the "hub."
💻

Webinar

Live Engagement & Lead Generation

What It Is

A live or pre-recorded 30-45 minute session with Jonathan (or expert) covering a high-value topic, with time for Q&A. Used as a launch event or ongoing engagement tool.

How to Make It Valuable

  • Clear value prop: Promote specific takeaways attendees will get
  • Timed with launch: Schedule around course/checklist availability
  • Interactive elements: Polls, Q&A, chat to boost engagement
  • Slide deck: Share as downloadable resource post-webinar
  • Recording strategy: Offer replay to registrants who miss it
  • Follow-up sequence: Email attendees with course offer within 24 hours
  • Repurpose content: Cut into short clips for social and ads
Best Practice: Structure as 60% teaching, 20% Q&A, 20% soft pitch for course. Make the teaching so valuable that the course becomes a natural next step.
📊

Case Studies

Social Proof & Credibility

What It Is

Real customer success stories showing how Semble customers launched their practices. Can be written, video interviews, or mixed media.

How to Make It Valuable

  • Problem-Solution-Result format: Clear narrative arc
  • Specific metrics: Time saved, patients seen, revenue impact
  • Quote-heavy: Let customer voice come through authentically
  • Visual elements: Photos of practice, screenshots of Semble in action
  • Multiple formats: Long-form written + short video testimonial
  • Hub integration: Feature case studies prominently on relevant hub sections
  • Specialty diversity: Show different practice types (GP, dental, aesthetics)
Best Practice: Focus case studies on the transformation journey, not just Semble features. Show how they went from "stuck at registration" to "thriving practice."
📱

Social Content

Organic Reach & Community Building

What It Is

Short-form posts, carousels, video clips, and stories shared on LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook promoting hub content, blog posts, and campaign assets.

How to Make It Valuable

  • Snippet strategy: Pull key insights from blogs/videos as standalone posts
  • Carousel posts: Turn blog sections into visual carousels (LinkedIn loves these)
  • Question posts: Ask community questions, link to hub for answers
  • Behind-the-scenes: Show Jonathan filming, course creation, customer wins
  • Consistent posting: 3-5x/week to maintain visibility
  • Engagement focus: Comment on related posts, build community
  • Always link back: Every post should drive to hub, blog, or course
Best Practice: Create social content in batches by repurposing existing assets. One blog post = 5-7 social posts. One video = 10+ clips/quotes.

Email Sequences

Nurture & Conversion Driver

What It Is

Automated email sequences triggered by course enrollment, webinar attendance, or hub downloads. Guides leads through their journey with valuable content and soft CTAs. Also serves as re-engagement tool for early-stage prospects who went quiet during CQC registration.

How to Make It Valuable

  • Welcome series: 3-5 emails introducing course, setting expectations
  • Progress nudges: Remind users to complete modules with specific value props
  • Value-first approach: Each email teaches something, not just promotes
  • Segmentation: Different sequences for course completers vs. non-starters vs. re-engaged prospects
  • Re-engagement for early prospects: Specific sequence for existing prospects who had demos but went quiet during CQC process
  • Sales handoff triggers: Alert sales when course completers show buying intent signals
  • Clear CTAs: One primary action per email
  • Timing optimization: Test send times for engagement
  • Re-engagement campaigns: Win back inactive course enrollees

Key Sequence Types

  • New prospect welcome: Standard onboarding for net-new course enrollees
  • Early-stage re-engagement: "We know you're going through CQC—here's how we can help" angle for existing prospects
  • Course completion: Congratulations + next steps (demo booking, case studies)
  • Non-starter nurture: Enrolled but haven't started—motivation and quick wins
  • Stalled progress: Started but haven't completed—case study or testimonial to drive completion
Best Practice: Map email sequences to user behavior AND their relationship with Semble. If they're an existing prospect who went quiet, acknowledge that context: "We know CQC registration can be overwhelming—that's why we created this resource to help you through it." This shows you understand their journey and positions Semble as a partner, not just a vendor.
