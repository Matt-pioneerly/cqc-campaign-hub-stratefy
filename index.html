📈 PHASED APPROACH

Pre-Phase 1: Content seeding & partner identification
  • Blog content creation and publishing
  • Social content distribution
  • Identify and secure partners for launch distribution (leverage their audiences at launch)
  • Secure commitments for campaign sharing
Phase 1: Hub Launch
  • Publish refreshed hub with comprehensive content
  • Continue seeding blogs and socials
Phase 2: Campaign Launch
  • Publish campaign (course/interactive checklist)
  • Launch distribution (including partner amplification)
  • Full campaign promotion
Phase 3: Enhancement
  • Enhance campaign assets
  • Enhance distribution efforts
  • Add more expert perspectives
  • Additional partner videos and contributions
Phase 4: Add video to hub and course
Phase 5: Repurpose videos in outreach
  • Leverage expanded expert/partner/customer networks
  • Partners and contributors share assets
Phase 6: Enhance hub with additional materials
  • Case studies, buyers guide
Phase 7: Evolve other hub sections into courses + other useful assets
